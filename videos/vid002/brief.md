# vid002 brief

- **Goal:** get students excited to join the Claude Builder Club.
- **Audience:** University of Ghana students.
- **Where:** social media, vertical.
- **Length:** 20 seconds.
- **Tone:** playful and thrilling in the ride, within the club's colours and type. Faster than vid001 on purpose.
- **Audio:** original generated music and effects, in `audio/score.mjs`.
- **Message:** "Join the Claude Builder Club and let's go for a ride."

Two Clawds talk in liquid glass bubbles. One meets the other holding a balloon and offers to do something fun. The camera pans to a roller coaster and they ride it, with wind and a close-up as they descend fast. The camera pulls out as they reach the top, then stays still as they leave the frame, and the message appears.
