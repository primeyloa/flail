# Lessons: flat people

Keep this under 40 lines.

- Keep heads small: about 1/6 of body height or less.
- Both arms and both legs must be visible at all times.
- Standing legs are straight and parallel, never a triangle.
- Each arm swings opposite the leg on the same side.
- Make gender and identity readable through distinctive hair and silhouettes.
- Keep raised hands clear of the face and of background shapes.
