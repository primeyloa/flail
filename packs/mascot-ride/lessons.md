# Lessons: mascot-ride

Keep this under 40 lines.

- Seat Clawds so their feet sit inside the car. In the first test they float above it.
- Keep the car slim. A thick body turns into a black slab in close-up.
- Keep balloon strings behind Clawd and wind lines behind the world.
- Keep text clear of dark lines behind glass. Use light background lines so bending shows without hurting legibility.
- Put a speech bubble over the Clawd who's speaking.
- Don't let same-coloured shapes cross: fade the background one.
- Wind belongs to camera movement. It stops when the camera stops.
