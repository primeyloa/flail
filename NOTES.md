## Liquid glass test (2026-10-31)

Chrome's `backdrop-filter: url(#svg-filter)` renders through HyperFrames and refracts a moving background, at normal render speed. The first attempt used a noise displacement map, which looks torn rather than like a lens. For a future pack: bend mostly at the edges with a generated map, drop `saturate` because it pushes coral to orange, and keep text clear of busy backgrounds.
