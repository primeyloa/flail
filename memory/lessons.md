# Lessons

Rules learned from feedback. Keep this under 40 lines: merge or remove old lines rather than growing it. Details are in `log.md`.

## Character

- Keep heads small: about 1/6 of body height or less.
- Both arms and both legs must be visible at all times.
- Standing legs are straight and parallel, never a triangle.
- Each arm swings opposite the leg on the same side.
- Make gender and identity readable through distinctive hair and silhouettes.

## Motion and feel

- The look is premium and calm, like the club's kit. Don't add energy.
- Hurried and rigid motion is the most likely complaint. Give motion more time and use continuous curves.

## Review

- Look at frames before showing the user. Check for hands crossing faces, shapes colliding with heads, and props that read as something else.
- Check the first frame: it should not be empty.
- Compare the frames either side of every cut.
- The "Needs changes" button can arrive with no details. Ask, don't guess.
