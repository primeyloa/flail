# Lessons for every pack

Rules learned from feedback. Keep this under 40 lines: merge or remove old lines rather than growing it. Details are in `log.md`. Rules about one animation type go in that pack's `lessons.md`.

## Feel

- The look is premium and calm, like the club's kit. Don't add energy unless the brief asks for it.
- Hurried and rigid motion is the most likely complaint. Give motion more time and use continuous curves.

## Review

- Look at frames before showing the user. Check for elements colliding, and props that read as something else.
- Check the first frame: it should not be empty.
- Compare the frames either side of every cut.
- The "Needs changes" button can arrive with no details. Ask, don't guess.
